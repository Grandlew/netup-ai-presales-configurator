# Hospitality Decision Tree

Hotel projects now follow a separate decision branch before the system presents a confident architecture.

## Priority questions

1. What TV brand and exact model or series will be installed?
2. Are the TVs hospitality/commercial models or retail TVs?
3. For LG, is `Pro:Centric Direct` confirmed?
4. Will room-TV delivery use Ethernet, Wi-Fi, coaxial, or a hybrid network?
5. Should mobile viewing work only on hotel Wi-Fi or also outside the property?
6. Is PMS integration required?
7. Is there existing headend equipment that should be retained?

## Decision effects

- Unknown TV compatibility keeps STB-free delivery conditional.
- Existing headend equipment enables a software-layer architecture option.
- Hybrid coaxial plus Wi-Fi can justify a branched room-TV and interactive-services path.
- PMS requirements raise the priority of hospitality-specific NetUP options.
